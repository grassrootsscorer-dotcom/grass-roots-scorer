Privacy Policy

Grass Roots Matchday Scorer · Last updated 16 October 2026

Grass Roots Matchday Scorer is an app for coaches to record team squads, match results, goals, assists, cards and penalties at youth football matches. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app. In short: your data stays on your phone, and we do not collect it.

Who we are

The app is provided by Iain Strickland. You can contact us at iain@neuromodmarketaccess.com.

What the app stores

When you use the app you may enter team names, player names and squad numbers, opposition names, referee names and ratings, and match events (goals, assists, cards, penalties and lineups). Some of this relates to children. All of it is saved only on your own device.

What we do not do

  • We do not have accounts, logins or a server that holds your match data.
  • We do not receive, copy, view or store the information you enter.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools, and we do not sell or share your data.

Information that leaves your phone

  • Backups and sharing, only when you choose. The Backup & Restore screen creates a file containing your data and opens your phone's share sheet so you can save it (for example to iCloud Drive or Files) or send it. The Share button on a match summary shares the match text you choose to send. Once you save or send a file, where it goes is up to you, and its privacy is governed by the service you choose.
  • App updates. The app can download improvements through Expo's update service. When it checks for an update, standard technical information (such as your device's IP address, app version and operating system) is sent to Expo as part of the request. No match, team or player data is sent. See Expo's privacy policy.
  • App stores. Apple (App Store and TestFlight) and, for Android, Google handle downloading and installing the app under their own privacy policies.

Children's information

Coaches may record the names of children in their squad. If you use the app, you are responsible for making sure you are entitled to record that information, for example by agreement with your club and the players' parents or carers. We recommend using first names only or initials. The app is designed for use by coaches (adults); it is not directed at children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete teams, players and matches inside the app, which removes them from the app's storage, or delete the app, which removes all of its data. Backup files you have saved elsewhere are not removed automatically; delete them yourself where you saved them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on your behalf, and you control it directly in the app. If you have any question about this policy or your rights under UK data protection law, contact us at the address above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, for example by adding cloud storage or team sharing, we will update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect.
