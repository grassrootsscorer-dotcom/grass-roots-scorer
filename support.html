Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. There are no accounts and no cloud storage, so the app works without signal at the pitch. It also means that if you lose or replace your phone, your data goes with it unless you have made a backup.

How do I back up my data?

Home screen → Backup & Restore → Backup Data, then save the file to iCloud Drive, Files or email it to yourself. The app also offers a backup when you finish a match.

How do I move to a new phone?

Make a backup on the old phone, get the file onto the new phone, install the app, then go to Backup & Restore → Restore and choose the file. Restoring replaces everything currently in the app.

Can I set up a match before I get to the pitch?

Yes. On New Match, choose a date and tap Save for Later. Find it under Match Centre → Upcoming.

Can I add a match I've already played?

Yes. In Match Centre, tap Add completed match data. You can record goals, assists and cards for each period without exact times.

The clock or screen stopped during a match.

The match clock keeps correct time even if the screen locks. The app asks your phone to keep the screen awake during a live match. If something looks wrong, email us and we will help.

How do I delete my data?

Delete teams, players or matches inside the app, or delete the app, which removes everything it stores. Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted by you. See the Privacy Policy.

Who can see my team's data?

Only you. We do not receive it.
